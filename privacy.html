🧪 Potion Fusion — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-08-25 · Contact: amostal10@gmail.com

The short version

Potion Fusion works fully offline. Analytics is off until you opt in, anonymous when on, and can be turned off any time in the in-game shop. Signing in is optional. We never sell data.

What the game stores on your device

Progress (levels, coins, boosters, your aquarium fish) is saved locally on your device. It never leaves your device unless a feature below is enabled.

Anonymous analytics (opt-in)

On first launch the game asks whether you want to share anonymous gameplay statistics (levels played, moves, coins earned/spent, booster and feature usage, app version, coarse platform type). If you accept, events are sent to our database hosted by Supabase keyed by a random install identifier — not your name, email, contacts, precise location, or advertising ID, none of which are collected. You can switch this off at any time: Shop → “Anonymous analytics … tap to turn off.” Opting out also discards anything queued.

Optional account sign-in

You can play as a guest forever. If you choose to sign in with Google or Facebook (via Supabase Auth), we receive your basic profile (name, email, avatar) to identify your account, and — if analytics is on — your gameplay events are linked to your account id so your progress insights survive reinstalls. Sign out at any time from the profile panel.

Rewarded ads

Ads in Potion Fusion are only ever rewarded videos you choose to watch — there are no forced or banner ads. In builds that include Google AdMob, Google may process device information (including advertising identifiers) to serve and measure ads under Google's Privacy Policy; consent is requested where required by law.

Data retention & deletion

Analytics events are retained for product-improvement purposes and periodically pruned. To have data tied to your install id or account deleted, email the contact above from the account in question (or include your install id from the support screen) and we will remove it within 30 days.

Children

The game is designed for a general audience and collects no personal data without opt-in. If you believe a child has provided personal data, contact us and we will delete it.

Changes

We will update this page when practices change; material changes are announced in-game.
